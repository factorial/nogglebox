# Curriculum

Nogglebox aims to be what Sesame Street was: not only letters and numbers, but the whole range of what a pre-K child is learning, including feelings, getting along, the natural world, community, health, music, art and other languages.

This file is the map: the subject areas, what each one teaches, ideas for pages, and what exists so far. The areas roughly follow the domains early-childhood programs use (e.g. the Head Start Early Learning Outcomes Framework), so the site covers what preschools cover.

## How pages are tagged
Each entry in `pages.js` lists the areas it teaches:

```js
{ id: 7, title: "Math Box", areas: ["math"] },
```

Area keys: `letters`, `math`, `feelings`, `thinking`, `science`, `community`, `health`, `music`, `art`, `languages`.
A page can have several areas. The harness ignores the tags; they exist to keep the mix balanced, and later to build episodes that mix areas.

## Principles
- **Video first.** At least half of all pages should be short videos (animations, musicals, interviews with children, puppets talking with adults; think 70s Sesame Street). Interactive pages are a surprising change of pace, not the norm. Each area should still mix formats.
- **Repetition across pages.** The same idea (a letter, a feeling, an animal) coming back in different activities is how it sticks.
- **No instructions needed.** Kids should learn what to do just by playing: make the first touch do something fun and obvious, and let the page teach through what happens. Very basic phonics reading is fine (it's part of what Nogglebox teaches), but never rely on written or spoken instructions.
- **Representation.** Diverse faces, families, voices and names throughout.

## Language & literacy (`letters`)
**Goals:** recognize letters (upper and lower case), match letters to sounds, blend sounds into words, rhyme, build vocabulary, listen to and retell stories.
**Have:** Letter Tracing (4), Sound It Out (5).
**Ideas:**
- Lowercase tracing; matching "Aa" pairs
- Rhyme time: which picture rhymes with "cat"?
- First-sound hunt: tap everything that starts with "b"
- Read-along picture stories with words lighting up as they're read
- Story order: put 3 pictures in the right order

## Math & numbers (`math`)
**Goals:** count to 20, recognize numerals, one-to-one counting, more/less/same, simple adding and taking away, shapes, sizes, measuring, patterns.
**Have:** Falling Numbers (1), Shape Sides (3), Math Box (7).
**Ideas:**
- More or less: which side has more?
- Number line hop: hop the bunny forward 3
- Big, bigger, biggest: order by size
- Shape hunt: find the circles in a picture
- Measuring with blocks: how many blocks long is the snake?

## Feelings & getting along (`feelings`)
**Goals:** name feelings (happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised, proud); recognize them in faces; calm-down strategies; sharing, taking turns, kindness, saying sorry; empathy.
**Have:** nothing yet; the biggest gap.
**Ideas:**
- Feelings faces: build a face, hear and name the feeling
- How would you feel? Short scenes ("your tower fell down") and pick the face
- Belly breathing: smell the flower, blow out the candle, with a calming animation
- Sharing scene: split the cookies so everyone gets one
- Kindness videos: helping a friend, taking turns on the slide

## Thinking skills (`thinking`)
**Goals:** sorting and classifying, patterns, same and different, opposites, memory, sequencing, simple cause and effect, problem solving.
**Have:** nothing yet.
**Ideas:**
- One of these things is not like the others
- Sorting: drag animals to land or water, foods to fruit or vegetable
- Pattern train: red, blue, red, blue… what comes next?
- Opposites flip: tap to flip big/small, up/down, hot/cold, open/closed
- Memory match cards
- Simple mazes and "what happens next?" scenes

## Science & nature (`science`)
**Goals:** animals (sounds, homes, babies, what they eat); plants and growing; weather and seasons; the senses; day and night; materials (sink/float, hard/soft); simple experiments and noticing.
**Have:** nothing yet.
**Ideas:**
- Animal sounds: tap an animal to hear it, then guess who made the sound
- Who lives here? Match animals to homes (nest, burrow, pond)
- Grow a plant: water it, add sun, watch it grow
- Dress for the weather: rain, snow, sunshine
- Sink or float: drop things into water and see
- Color mixing: blue + yellow = ?
- Real-world videos: caterpillar to butterfly, ice melting

## Community & the world (`community`)
**Goals:** helpers and jobs, places in a neighborhood, families of all kinds, vehicles and how people get around, how things are made, cultures and celebrations.
**Have:** nothing yet.
**Ideas:**
- Community helpers: who puts out fires? Who helps when you're sick?
- Build a town: place the bakery, park, school and fire station
- Vehicles and their sounds; what goes on roads, rails, water and sky
- "How it's made" videos (crayons, bread, a bicycle), in the spirit of Sesame Street's factory films
- Families: many kinds of families, homes and celebrations

## Health & body (`health`)
**Goals:** body parts, washing hands, brushing teeth, healthy foods, sleep, staying active, safety basics.
**Have:** nothing yet.
**Ideas:**
- Handwashing song with a 20-second timer
- Brush the monster's teeth
- Body parts: touch your nose! Point to the knee
- Everyday foods and sometimes foods
- Movement breaks: jump like a frog, stretch like a cat
- Bedtime routine sequencing

## Music & movement (`music`)
**Goals:** singing, rhythm and beat, loud and soft, fast and slow, high and low, instruments and their sounds, moving to music.
**Have:** Piano (6).
**Ideas:**
- Sing-alongs with words lighting up (alphabet song, counting songs)
- Copy the rhythm: hear a clap pattern, tap it back
- Instrument guessing: which instrument made that sound?
- Freeze dance: dance, and stop when the music stops
- Make-a-band: drums, shakers and bells layered together

## Art & creativity (`art`)
**Goals:** drawing and coloring, colors, making things, pretend play, self-expression.
**Have:** Sand Drawing (2).
**Ideas:**
- Coloring pages with tap-to-fill
- Stamp art with shapes and animals
- Make a face or a monster from parts
- Dress-up and pretend: chef, astronaut, vet

## World languages (`languages`)
**Goals:** hearing that people speak many languages; simple words and greetings; counting in other languages (for example Spanish, Japanese, and the languages our families speak).
**Have:** nothing yet.
**Ideas:**
- Hello around the world: tap a friend to hear "hello" in their language
- Counting 1–10 in Japanese and Spanish
- Word of the day in another language, tied to the letter or number of the day
- Animal names in several languages
